The beginning

Why We Started LumaStory

The world already has thousands of children’s books. Many are entertaining. Many are educational. Many are beautifully illustrated.

We wanted to create something different.

Stories children genuinely love today. Stories they still remember years later. Stories that become part of family conversations long after the last page.

What we believe

The quiet ideas behind every story

These are not rules for children. They are promises we make to the stories we create.

01

Stories Before Lessons

Children discover ideas more deeply than they receive them.

02

Wonder Before Explanation

Curiosity creates lasting learning. Not instruction.

03

Characters Before Messages

Children return because they love the characters. Meaning follows naturally.

04

Families Read Together

Every story is written for two readers: the child, and the adult beside them.

05

Small Ideas Become Lifelong Memories

Sometimes one sentence stays with a child forever.
